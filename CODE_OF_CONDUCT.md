# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about the code, not the person.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Standards

Things that make this project better:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people new to AVD, Azure, or open source
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully, and giving it the same way
- Focusing on what is best for people running this software
- Saying "I do not know" instead of guessing, particularly about Microsoft or Nerdio APIs

Things that are not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission — this includes posting tenant
  identifiers, customer names or user names from a real environment in an issue
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate.

Report problems by opening a private security advisory or contacting a maintainer directly.
Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter's privacy is respected.
